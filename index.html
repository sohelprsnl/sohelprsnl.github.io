How I work

Beyond order follow-up.

A price is only real if the yarn behind it is real. A delivery date is only real if the line can run it. Most of what goes wrong in an order is visible weeks earlier, in a fabric booking, a capacity commitment or an approval that has not moved, and the value of the job is catching it there rather than at final inspection.

That means working with two sets of constraints at once. Buyers need specification, margin and the date. Factories need lead time, material readiness and a realistic load. Finding the answer that holds at both ends is the part that takes fourteen years to learn.
