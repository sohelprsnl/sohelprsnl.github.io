About me

Fourteen years, every link of the chain.

From assistant merchandiser on a factory floor in Mymensingh to leading merchandising teams for European retailers in Dhaka, and now, into the AI era of apparel.

My mission

Help garment manufacturing get smarter, without losing what makes it work.

“The next decade of RMG belongs to people who pair floor experience with technology. I intend to be one of them, and to bring others along.”

I started as an assistant merchandiser at Crystal Martin Apparel in 2011, chasing lab dips and preparing costings with the production floor right downstairs. That grounding set the rule I still work by: never quote a price I cannot trace back to yarn, and never promise a delivery date without knowing what the factory can actually run.

Everything since has been the same job at a larger scale. Today I lead a four-person merchandising team at LPP S.A.’s Bangladesh liaison office, covering denim for Cropp, House, and Mohito and accessories for all five LPP brands. Before that came Celio and New Wave Group.

Alongside the day job, I study and I write. The industry is changing faster than most sourcing playbooks. I would rather write the new one than be surprised by it.

Three things at once
